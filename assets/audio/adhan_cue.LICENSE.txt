adhan_cue.wav is a format conversion (Ogg Vorbis to 16-bit PCM WAV, mono, 22050 Hz) of that recording so iOS and Android can use it as a bundled notification sound. The content of the recording is unchanged. Duration is under 30 seconds.
This cue is not the voice the user selects for the full adhan. The full recordings stay online.
